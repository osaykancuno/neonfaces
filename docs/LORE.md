# NEONFACES — Lore & Manifesto

**Name:** NEONFACES · **Symbol:** NEON · **Supply:** 5555
**Tagline:** They don't blink.
**Subtitle:** 5555 close-up faces. Every one is an account.

## Short lore (landing, threads)

On Robinhood Chain the market never closes. Someone has to keep watching.

NEONFACES are not portraits of people. They are the faces of agents. No bodies — they don't need them: an eye, a nose, a strip of forehead. Everything else is out of frame. The crop is this tight because the only thing that matters is the ticker.

Every Face is born with a Token Bound Account. Inside that wallet lives a piece of the real market: Stock Tokens, USDG, whatever the holder or the agent puts there. Sell the Face and you sell the stare — and everything it has accumulated.

They don't smile. They don't pose. They don't blink.

## Extended lore (about / manifesto)

First there were portraits. Then PFPs. Then pixel brokers with stocks in their pockets.

NEONFACES strips the costume. What's left is zero distance: skin reduced to blocks, light reduced to #CCFF00, identity reduced to an account.

5555 is not random. Five, like the fingers holding a phone open all night. Five thousand and more, because a 24/7 market needs more than one watcher.

Some faces are darker. Some carry more neon. That's not cosmetic rarity: it's how long that stare has stayed on.

The chain's agents can be delegated to a Face's account. The holder stays the owner. The stare can keep working while you sleep.

## Native to the chain — three things, no jargon

1. **Every Face has its own wallet.** Not an image sitting in an account: the image is the key to an account. Tokens, stablecoins and pieces of tokenized stocks can live inside it.
2. **A Face is never left empty.** The mint pays for what's inside: part of every sale can only buy a small, visible basket for the Faces, different from piece to piece. Most Faces are born with it; one minted before the pool is restocked gets it minutes later. The holder can leave it there, add to it, or withdraw it. If the Face changes hands, what's inside follows.
3. **The stare can work.** The chain is built for agents too. The holder stays the owner and can delegate simple rules ("keep part of it liquid", "accumulate one ticker"). Not a landing-page bot: the same idea as a face that never closes its eyes.

**Mandatory clarity:** we don't sell real shares or shareholder rights. We sell an artwork that can hold on-chain exposure. The language stays honest — otherwise the project dies at the first serious question.

If the Faces were born empty, NEONFACES would be just an aesthetic. The aesthetic is strong. It isn't enough.

## How this lasts — and how it fails

**It works if**
- the art stays iron-clad and recognizable
- the Face lit at mint is real, not a slide
- the community is chosen, not inflated
- the tone stays adult: market, stare, neon — no mascots

**It fails if**
- it becomes "yet another pixel broker"
- it promises returns
- it grows the supply or the allowlist out of fear (the supply is 5555 in bytecode — it can't grow)
- the team disappears after mint and only the listings feed is left

The reference isn't the biggest collection on the chain. It's the one that proved a portrait can carry a piece of the market. NEONFACES takes that intuition and moves it to art: less character, more close-up, the chain's color, a stare instead of a costume.

## Tone rules for every channel

- Say "on-chain exposure held in an account bound to the Face". Never "shares inside an NFT", never "dividends", never "returns".
- Every post that mentions the basket carries: *Stock Tokens give economic exposure only, not legal ownership, and are not available to US persons.*
- No mascots, no emojis in the brand voice, no countdown hype. Market, stare, neon.
