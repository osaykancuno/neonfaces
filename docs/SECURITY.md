# Security review

Internal review, 2026-09-25: second pass after the move to OpenSea and the self-funding seed vault, third pass (pre-mint audit) after sets, agents and strategies. It is not a substitute for an independent audit, which the treasury will pay for once the mint has funded it.

Re-run 2026-09-26, after the final art: 119 tests in the CI profile (fuzz 5,000 runs, invariants 256 × 100) and the 7 mainnet-fork tests pass; Slither 0.11.5 gives 260 results in `src/`, all in the classes triaged below (the `reentrancy-eth` it reports on `NeonPayout.releaseAll` is a false positive: `release` is `nonReentrant`, updates its accounting before sending and pays only the four immutable payees); `forge lint` shows the same warning classes as I-06 and I-13; no new finding. Tracked files and git history hold none of the keys in the git-ignored `.env` files.

**Scope:** `contracts/src/*` (NeonFaces, NeonPayout, NeonSeedVault, NeonSeeder, NeonFaceAccount, NeonArt, NeonRenderer, NeonTrader, ChainEntropy), the SeaDrop integration, deploy/upload scripts, site transaction flows.
**Method:** line-by-line manual review with an attacker's model; Slither 0.11.5 static analysis (137 results, every one triaged, see I-13); invariant fuzzing (4 invariants over random sequences of sales, team mints, transfers, payouts, pauses, reveal and top-ups: 25,600 calls in the CI profile, no violation); 102 Foundry tests (6 of them on a mainnet fork) including attack scenarios (tier re-roll contract, rogue agent, front-run drain, a set emptied from the inside after a locked sale, expensive routes, pool withdrawals, full-supply mapping, whole sets on any sale size, ownership cycles, set bonus replay, sale-manager limits, fee routing, payout redirection); every test mint goes through OpenSea's real SeaDrop bytecode; mainnet-fork tests with the live SeaDrop, real Stock Tokens, the real Uniswap v3 router/pools and Chainlink feeds; `forge lint` (every warning reviewed); end-to-end rehearsal on a local node with the canonical registry and SeaDrop bytecode, including the holder panel and a live agent.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| H-01 | High | **Tier sniping.** The Stare tier was drawn inside the mint transaction; a contract could mint, read its tier and revert until it got Heavy Stare (the largest basket), paying only gas. | **Fixed.** Nothing valuable is decided at mint: every Face gets an equivalent base basket. Tier and art are set together at reveal by a keyed permutation; Watch / Heavy Stare top-ups are delivered after reveal (permissionless). Test: `test_Security_TierCannotBeSnipedAtMint`. |
| H-02 | High | **Agents trading directly on a DEX router.** Selector-level permissions can't constrain arguments: an agent allowed `exactInput` could set itself as `recipient`, or accept any price and sandwich its own trades to drain the Face. | **Fixed.** Ready-made actions only allow `NeonTrader.swap`: output always paid to the calling Face, minimum output from Chainlink (≤ 1% + pool fees, at most 2% in total), listed tokens only, stale prices refused, daily USD cap set by the holder. Verified on a mainnet fork against the real Uniswap router, pools and feeds (`TraderFork.t.sol`). |
| H-03 | High | **Mint money routed around the split through fees** (found in this pass). The sale manager could allow its own fee recipient and set a stage fee up to 100%; anyone minting directly on SeaDrop with that recipient would send the whole price there and nothing to `NeonPayout`. Unrestricted fee recipients would also let any minter name itself and get a rebate. | **Fixed.** `NeonFaces` refuses public, token-gated and signed stages with a fee above `MAX_FEE_BPS` (10%, OpenSea's) or without restricted fee recipients. Allowlist stages carry their terms inside the Merkle leaves, which the contract can't read: see I-09. Test: `test_Studio_FeesCannotRouteMoneyAroundThePayout`. |
| M-01 | Medium | **Resale front-run.** A seller could empty the Face account after listing, just before the sale executes; the buyer receives an empty Face. | **Fixed.** `lock(until)` on the account blocks holder calls, agent calls and ERC-1271 signatures; it survives the sale and shows as "Locked Until" in the metadata. Tests: `test_Lock_*`. |
| M-02 | Medium | **Late minters could pick art.** If the reveal happened while minting was open, the permutation would be known for future token ids. | **Fixed.** `requestReveal()` closes minting forever (`mintClosed`), team mint included. |
| M-03 | Medium | **Reveal grinding.** The metadata role could let a reveal window expire and request again. | **Mitigated.** No re-request while the target block is pending or its hash is readable; every request is counted on-chain (`revealRequests`) and evented; `tools/reveal-watch.mjs` lets anyone finalize immediately. Residual: a deliberately missed 25 s window can be retried, visibly. |
| M-05 | Medium | **Faces locked inside themselves** (found while adding sets). A Face sent with plain `transferFrom` into its own account, or into the account of a Face it holds (A inside B while B is inside A), could never move again: its only controller would be itself. Solady's `SelfOwnDetected` check runs only on safe transfers. Sets make nesting common. | **Fixed.** `NeonFaces._update` walks up the chain of Face accounts above the receiver and reverts `OwnershipCycle` if it reaches the Face being moved, or after 4 levels. A contract that only pretends to be a Face account can at worst refuse Faces sent to itself. Tests: `test_Cycle_*`. |
| M-06 | Medium | **A set sold as one Face could be emptied from the inside** (found in the pre-mint pass). Before listing an assembled anchor, a seller could, through the anchor's account, delegate an agent on a piece's account (or approve an operator on a piece), then lock the anchor. The piece accounts had their own lock and their agents were bound to their direct owner (the anchor's account, unchanged by the sale): after the sale, or even during the listing, the agent drained the pieces' baskets and the operator pulled pieces out, while the anchor showed "Locked Until". | **Fixed.** `NeonFaceAccount` walks up the Face accounts above it: `holder()` is the holder of the outermost Face (agents are bound to it, so they die when the outer Face is sold) and `effectiveLockedUntil()` is the latest lock on the way (a piece inside a locked Face is locked: no holder call, agent call or signature). `NeonFaces` refuses any transfer out of a locked Face account (`FaceAccountLocked`), so an operator approved before the lock can't pull pieces out; the check fails closed. Deliberate stacks deeper than 8 levels count as locked with no holder until taken apart from the top. Metadata, site, MCP kit and strategy agent read the effective lock. Tests: `test_Nested_*`. |
| M-07 | Medium | **Admin could still add a minter during the sale** (pre-mint pass). `updateAllowedSeaDrop` let the admin allow any address to call `mintSeaDrop` and mint the unsold public allocation for free. | **Fixed.** The list of allowed SeaDrop contracts is fixed at the first mint (`MintAlreadyStarted`); pausing still works. Test: `test_Security_NoNewMinterOnceTheSaleStarted`. |
| M-08 | Medium | **Pool inventory owed to Faces was withdrawable** (pre-mint pass). `withdrawPool` had no limit, and the vault's ETH could go to the treasury while seeds, top-ups or set bonuses were still unpaid: "the admin may withdraw unused inventory" was a promise the code didn't keep. | **Fixed.** Baskets can only be locked after the reveal; from then on `withdrawPool` can't touch `owed(token)` (pending base seeds, undelivered Watch / Heavy Stare top-ups, unpaid set bonuses, each at the largest leg of its tier, tiers counted by `tierCounts()` from the reveal permutation) and `releaseSurplus` needs `covered()` (the pool holds everything owed), or 180 days after the lock if a token can no longer be bought. Tests: `test_Seeder_OwedStaysInThePoolAfterLock`, `test_Surplus*`. |
| M-09 | Medium | **Expensive routes widened an agent's price** (pre-mint pass). The minimum output subtracted every pool fee on the path: an agent (or a stolen strategy-agent key) could route a Face through three 1% pools it seeded itself and take about 4% of each trade. | **Fixed.** Pool fees count at most `MAX_ROUTE_FEE_BPS` (1%): with the 1% slippage cap, no trade clears more than 2% under Chainlink. Real routes cost 0.01% to 0.6%. Test: `test_Route_ExpensivePoolsCantWidenThePrice`. |
| M-04 | Medium | **Gas drain in entropy probing** (found in rehearsal). Calling an emulated ArbSys that hits INVALID consumed 63/64 of the transaction gas. | **Fixed.** Precompile calls are gas-capped and ArbSys is used only if both `arbBlockNumber` and `arbBlockHash` work. |
| L-01 | Low | Minting before the provenance commitment would make the art impossible to seal. | **Fixed.** Mint reverts with `ProvenanceNotSet`. |
| L-02 | Low | Deploy script revoked the metadata role from the admin when `ADMIN == deployer`. | **Fixed.** |
| L-03 | Low (functional) | Agents could not spend ETH (no ETH → token strategies), no batching, permissions only replaceable wholesale. | **Fixed.** ETH budget (`setAgentValueAllowance`), `executeBatchAsAgent`, `setAgentPermissions(add/remove)`. |
| I-01 | Info | ERC-20 allowances granted *before* a lock stay valid at the token level (a lock can't reach into token contracts). | Documented in the contract, the holder panel and here. The Face page lists open token approvals on the account and on set pieces inside it ("Open token approvals"), so a buyer sees them before buying; NeonTrader is left out (it only trades for the account that calls it). Faces approved before a lock can't leave (M-06). |
| I-02 | Info | Allowing `transfer` / `approve` / `setApprovalForAll` to an agent lets it move assets. | By design (the holder chooses). The holder panel flags these selectors and asks for confirmation. |
| I-03 | Info | Randomness is L2 block-hash based, not a VRF. | Accepted: counts are exact by construction; only the reveal key depends on it. |
| I-04 | Info | Base-basket choice at mint is re-rollable. | Accepted: base baskets must be configured with equal value (documented in `config/baskets.plan.json`). |
| I-05 | Info | NeonTrader trusts Chainlink; there is no L2 sequencer-uptime feed on Robinhood Chain. | Accepted: prices older than 26 h are refused; the worst an agent can do inside the rules is 2% per trade, bounded by the holder's daily cap. |
| I-06 | Info | Lint: calls in loops, events after calls, `encodePacked` with dynamic args, ETH sends. | Reviewed: trusted immutable targets, strings only (never hashed), sends only to immutable payees or within the agent budget. |
| I-07 | Info | **Sale manager.** The wallet that runs the drop in OpenSea Studio can set stages, prices and allowlists (e.g. open a free public stage). | By design, narrowed on-chain: it cannot mint by itself, change the art, royalties or roles, allow another minter, or send proceeds anywhere but `NeonPayout`. The admin can pause minting and remove it; it is cleared after the sale. Test: `test_Studio_SaleManagerPowersAreNarrow`. |
| I-08 | Info | **Seed vault keeper.** A hot key triggers the seed purchases. | By design, narrowed on-chain: it can only make `NeonSeedVault` buy tokens used by a basket, through NeonTrader (Chainlink-bounded, ≤ 1%), delivered to the pool. A stolen keeper key can at worst buy the wrong mix of basket tokens, or sandwich its own buys for at most 2% of each purchase (it can only buy, so each ETH is spent once); the admin revokes it. Tests: `SeedVault.t.sol`, `test_Fork_SeedVaultBuysBasketTokensIntoThePool`. |
| I-09 | Info | **Allowlist stage terms are trusted.** Presale price, fee and fee-recipient restriction live in the Merkle leaves OpenSea Studio builds; a malicious root could make a stage free or fee-routed. | Accepted for a solo founder (the sale manager is a dedicated MetaMask account used only in OpenSea Studio). The list stage (0.013 ETH, 3 per wallet, 24 h) and its CSV are checked in Studio before publishing; `tools/verify-drop.mjs` checks the public stage, payout, fees, signers and that an allowlist root is set. The list itself is not published (the preview's check reads hashes only), so the root can't be recomputed by outsiders: the stage terms are announced instead. |
| I-10 | Info | A minter can send just enough gas for the mint to succeed while the seed delivery inside `try` runs out of gas. | Only affects the minter's own Face: its seed stays pending and the keeper (or anyone) delivers it. |
| I-11 | Info | While a sale manager is set, it is `owner()` for OpenSea, so it also controls the collection's OpenSea settings (description, links, off-chain creator-earnings address). | Clear it after the sale (`safe-tx.mjs sale-manager none`); on-chain royalties (ERC-2981) stay with the admin. |
| I-12 | Info | `NeonTrader.quote` divides before multiplying (USD value rounded to 8 decimals). | At most one hundred-millionth of a dollar per trade, and it only loosens the minimum output by that amount. Kept for readability. |
| I-14 | Info | **Sets.** Whole sets only (`setsIn(n)` from the final supply), the four ids of a set more than n/64 apart for n ≥ 256 (nobody is dealt a finished set by minting a run), one bonus per set ever (`setBonus[setId]`), assembly defined by live ownership. | Tests: `test_Sets_*`, `testFuzz_Sets_PartialSaleOnlyWholeSets`, `testFuzz_RevealKeyIsABijectionKey`. A buyer of an assembled Face relies on the seller not pulling pieces out before the sale lands: same as M-01, the account lock covers it ("Locked Until"). |
| I-15 | Info | `refreshMetadata()` is permissionless. | Rate-limited to once a day and only emits an event (no state that matters). |
| I-16 | Info | Optimizer runs lowered from 1000 to 200 to keep `NeonRenderer` (Gaze) under EIP-170. | Mint costs slightly more gas, cents at Robinhood Chain prices; every test re-run on the new build. |
| I-17 | Info | **Strategy agent key** (`RUNNER_PK`, a hot key on GitHub Actions). | Bounded like any agent: only `NeonTrader.swapWithNote` on Faces that delegated to it, fair price (at most 2% under Chainlink), output back into the Face, the holder's daily cap. Strategies are instructions stored by the holder, not permissions. Holders revoke in one click; the team rotates the key. Tests: `Strategies.t.sol`. |
| I-18 | Info | **Action links** (`/face/<id>?do=...`, prepared by AI assistants or anyone) could be used for phishing. | A link can only prepare: trades through NeonTrader, withdrawals to the connected holder's own wallet, locks, a strategy for the published strategy agent, or revoking the agent. It can't name a recipient or an agent; the page shows the action in plain words and nothing is sent without the holder's confirmation in their wallet. |
| I-19 | Info | **Reveal front-running.** The reveal seed is known as soon as the target block exists; anyone can compute every tier and set before marketplaces refresh. | Unavoidable for any on-chain reveal. The keeper and `reveal-watch.mjs` finalize at once, the site shows tiers immediately, and the runbook tells holders not to list at floor around the reveal. |
| I-20 | Info | **Deploy key window.** Until the Safe accepts the admin role (2-day delay) the deploy key is admin of every contract. | Operational: the Safe accepts before the sale opens; `verify-drop.mjs` now fails if any contract's admin isn't the Safe, a transfer is pending, or the deploy key kept any role (`UploadArt` renounces the artist role after sealing). |
| I-21 | Info | **Nesting through other collections.** A Face inside another collection's ERC-6551 account, itself held by the Face, is a cycle `NeonFaces` can't see. | Self-inflicted and exotic; safe transfers into Solady-based accounts still refuse it. |
| I-22 | Info | **Renderer JSON.** A basket token's symbol went unescaped into a trait name ("Holds X"). | Fixed: trait names are escaped like values. |
| I-23 | Info | **Keeper workflow.** Hot keys on GitHub Actions. | Schedule and manual triggers only (never pull requests), read-only token, `npm ci --ignore-scripts`. Keep 2FA on GitHub, Cloudflare, Porkbun and OpenSea: a hijacked site or domain is the most likely real attack on holders. |
| I-24 | Info | **Gold feed.** Chainlink's GLD / USD feed on Robinhood Chain is derived from DEX state, not from the ETF's market price: moving the gold pools moves the price NeonTrader checks against. | Accepted for small amounts: the pools are deep ($1.8M + $3.2M against USDG, pool price within 0.2% of the feed at listing), the seed vault's gold purchases total about $2,200, and a manipulator would pay arbitrage on millions to skew trades of a few dollars. Re-check with `tools/rwa-scan.mjs` before deploy; the other listed feeds are market reference prices. |
| I-25 | Info | **Set bonus inside a transfer.** Moving the last piece of a set into the anchor's account makes `NeonFaces._update` call `NeonSeeder.claimSetBonus` (external call during a transfer), so the bonus never waits for the keeper. | Accepted: the seeder is fixed once before the first mint and only moves basket tokens (ERC-20, no receiver hooks) into the anchor's account; the claim is `nonReentrant` and pays once per set. It runs on a fixed 1M gas budget (≈ 6 × what it uses) and the transfer reverts when that budget isn't available, so a wallet's gas estimate can't land on a limit where the piece moves in and the bonus is silently skipped; any other failure (a short pool) leaves the transfer valid and the bonus claimable by anyone. Tests: `test_Sets_LastPieceNeverSkipsTheBonusForLackOfGas`, `test_Sets_ShortPoolNeverBlocksAssembly`. |
| I-26 | Info | **Anyone can restock the pool.** `NeonSeedVault.restock` spends the vault's ETH on request of any caller, so no delivery depends on the keeper. | Accepted: it buys only the token the pool is short of, only up to what minted Faces are owed (`NeonSeeder.owed` minus the pool balance), at most $2,000 per call, sized from Chainlink, with NeonTrader's minimum output (≤ 1% slippage, route fees capped at 1%). The worst a caller can do is sandwich one call for ≤ 2% of it, or pick the more expensive listed route; each call shrinks the shortfall, so repeating it can't overspend. Surplus rules are unchanged. Tests: `test_AnyoneRestocksWhatIsOwedAndNoMore`, `test_RestockIsCappedPerCall`, fork `test_Fork_SeedVaultBuysBasketTokensIntoThePool`. |
| I-27 | Info | **Fused sets.** `NeonSeeder.fuse` makes a set's pieces immovable out of the anchor's account for good; `NeonFaces._update` asks the seeder on every move out of a Face account. | Accepted: only the anchor's direct owner can fuse, only an assembled set, once; the anchor itself stays freely transferable (the set moves whole). The check runs only after the reveal and only on moves out of a Face account. ERC-20 baskets in the pieces' accounts are not frozen by fusing (a lock covers them). Tests: `test_Fuse_*`. |
| I-28 | Info | **Set votes.** `NeonSetVotes` is advisory and holds nothing. | One vote per set id per poll, cast by `faces.ownerOf(anchor)` while `isAssembled`; changing it moves the vote, never adds one; reassembling around another piece keeps the same set id; only the immutable pollster (the Safe) can ask. Tests: `SetVotesTest`. |
| I-29 | Info | **One-transaction delivery.** `NeonSeedVault.restockAndDeliver(action, tokenId, paths, fees)` restocks every token in `paths` the pool is short of (tokens already held are skipped), then calls the seeder's permissionless `fund`, `upgrade` or `claimSetBonus`. A refused swap (Uniswap's `Error(string)`, e.g. "Too little received" when a pool is more than 1% off its feed after fees) becomes `MarketOffPrice(token)`; other reverts bubble. | Accepted: same limits as `restock` for each token (owed only, ≤ $2,000, Chainlink-bounded), the delivery goes to the Face's own account as always, and the call is all or nothing, so a failing leg buys nothing. Tests: `test_RestockAndDeliverInOneTransaction`, `test_MarketOffPriceIsNamed`. |
| I-30 | Info | **One-transaction assembly.** `NeonFaces.assembleSet(anchorId)` lets the holder of a set's anchor move the other pieces it holds into the anchor's account in one call (pieces already there are skipped). | Accepted: only the anchor's holder can call it, only pieces that holder owns move (`NotYourPiece` otherwise), and each move is an ordinary `_transfer` through `_update`, so cycles, locks, fused sets, events and the in-transfer set bonus behave exactly as with three separate transfers. Test: `test_Sets_AssembleInOneTransaction`. |
| I-13 | Info | Slither: 2 "High" `encode-packed-collision` in `NeonRenderer` (strings, never hashed), `unused-return`/`uninitialized-local` in the renderer's buffer code (intended), `calls-loop` to our own trusted contracts, `timestamp` for locks/expiry (seconds don't matter), `return-bomb` on a staticcall to the Face's own account (our immutable code). | Reviewed one by one: no change needed. |

## What holders do NOT have to trust

- **Supply**: 5555 total, 111 team, 5444 sale — constants in bytecode. Minting ends forever at the reveal request.
- **Art**: sealed in `NeonArt` only if it matches the provenance committed before the first mint (enforced).
- **Trading**: no transfer pause, no blocklist, no operator filter or transfer validator, no proxy. Royalty capped at 5% and optional.
- **Mint money**: SeaDrop can pay the creator share only to `NeonPayout`, and a marketplace fee of at most 10% only to restricted recipients (enforced by `NeonFaces` for public, token-gated and signed stages; allowlist stages see I-09). `NeonPayout`'s payees and shares are immutable; anyone can trigger the split; its 55% can only become seed-pool inventory (bought by the keeper, or by anyone for what Faces are owed).
- **Face accounts**: immutable implementation; only the current holder controls it; agents are scoped and die on sale, including the sale of a Face they sit inside; a lock covers the pieces inside.
- **New minters**: none can be added once the sale has started.

## What holders DO trust

| Area | Trust | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Seed pool | the seed share of the mint buys the inventory (`NeonSeedVault`), including set bonuses not yet claimed. Until the baskets are locked (after the reveal) the admin can change baskets and move inventory; after, only what exceeds `owed()` can leave, and the vault's ETH goes to the treasury only once the pool is `covered()` (M-08) | delivered seeds live in Face accounts; `owed()`, `covered()`, `coverage()`, `fundedCount()`, `upgradedCount()` are public |
| Reveal timing | the metadata role requests the reveal | M-03 above |
| Renderer | the metadata role can swap the renderer until `freezeMetadata()` | art bytes are sealed regardless; swaps emit ERC-4906 events |
| Sale configuration | the sale manager sets OpenSea stages, prices and allowlists; OpenSea runs SeaDrop and the drop page | I-07, H-03, I-09 above; `tools/verify-drop.mjs`; SeaDrop is OpenSea's audited, immutable contract |
| Stock Tokens | issued by third parties with their own rules | fork test proves transfers into Face accounts work; a blocked transfer leaves the seed *pending*, never breaks a mint |
