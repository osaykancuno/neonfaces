# Traits & exact rarities

Counts are fixed (exact decks), generated by `art/generate.py`, and published before mint. They are exact over the 5555 artworks; which of them are minted depends on the sale.

The 5555 artworks are **3335 single close-ups** and **555 sets of four pieces** (2220 artworks): one face cut into Left eye / Right eye / Left mouth / Right mouth, shown as the Crop of each piece. A set's pieces share every other trait and its Stare tier (444 Glance, 83 Watch, 28 Heavy Stare sets); Face is Woman (278 sets) or Man (277 sets); the Accessory shows only on the pieces where it lands; set pieces have no Anomaly. Each piece also carries **Set #n**.
Rule: shrink a Face to 48 px. If you can't tell it's a NEONFACE, the trait is too weak (`python art/generate.py` writes the test sheet to `art/output/legibility_48px.png`).
