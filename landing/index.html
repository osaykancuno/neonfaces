The list

      
Is your wallet on the list?

The idea

      
A portrait that holds something

      

        
On Robinhood Chain the market never closes, so someone has to keep watching. NEONFACES are the faces of those watchers: no bodies, no costumes, just an eye, a nose, a strip of forehead, cropped so tight that only the stare is left. Every one is drawn in blocks of neon #CCFF00 fading to black.

Plain truth
NEONFACES does not sell shares or shareholder rights, and promises no returns. It sells an artwork that can hold on-chain exposure. What's inside a Face is worth whatever those tokens are worth, and the holder can withdraw it at any time.

    

  


  

    

      
Art

      
The art lives on the chain

      

        
Most collections keep their images on a server or on IPFS, and the NFT only points at them. NEONFACES stores the pixels themselves on Robinhood Chain: every Face is a grid of 20 to 40 blocks, about 190 bytes, and the contract draws the image and writes its description every time someone asks. No server, no link that can break.

        
Every face starts from a black-and-white portrait of a person who doesn't exist, generated for the collection: 555 different people for the sets, others for the close-ups. Only the pixels made from them are stored, and they are the art.

        
The full set was sealed against a fingerprint published before the first mint, so nobody can swap the art later. There are twelve traits (crop, light, grain, expression, accessories and more) with exact counts over the 5555 artworks, published before the mint.

      

    

    

      
Stare tiers

      
Three kinds of stare, decided at the reveal

      

        
After the mint closes, the reveal assigns every token its art with a formula anyone can recompute on the chain. The art decides the tier: 4444, 833 and 278 among the 5555 artworks (if not every Face is minted, the shares stay the same on average). Watch and Heavy Stare Faces then receive a top-up on top of their base basket.

Nothing valuable is decided inside the mint transaction, so bots can't mint, peek at the tier and cancel until they land a rare one. Basket values are set from live prices right before launch.

    

    

      
The Gaze

      
Patience shows in the picture

      

        
Every Face counts how long it has stayed with its current holder. After 30, 90 and 365 days its neon starts to glow: the contract draws a glow into the art itself, so it shows on every marketplace and in every wallet. A sale resets it. The stare belongs to whoever keeps it; there is no payout for waiting, only the picture.

      

      

      
      
      
      
    

    

    

      
Live traits

      
Traits that keep watching

      

        
The metadata isn't frozen on day one. It reads each Face as it is now: how long it has stayed with its holder and the Gaze it earned, its set and whether it is assembled, what its wallet holds, and the date its lock opens.

      

    

  


  

    

      
Sets

      
Four pieces. One face.

      

        
555 faces were cut in four: left eye, right eye, left mouth, right mouth. Each piece is a Face of its own, with its own wallet. Half of the sets are women, half are men, and the four pieces of a set share the same tier.

  • Collect. The reveal deals whole sets only, so every set that exists can be completed, and the four pieces always land far apart: nobody gets a finished set by minting several Faces in a row. Each piece shows its set number and part, so you can make an offer on exactly the piece you miss.
    • 
      
  • Assemble. Move the other three pieces into one piece's wallet (one transaction from its page). That Face now shows the whole face, and whoever buys it gets all four pieces in one sale. Every piece stays a Face of its own: the supply stays 5555, and on OpenSea the three pieces show as held by that Face's wallet. Selling it? Lock it first, so buyers can see the pieces can't leave before the sale. You can take it apart whenever you like.
    • 
      
  • Hunt. The site's set hunter shows where the missing pieces are, who holds them and for how long, and tells you on your next visit which pieces of a set you watch changed hands.
    • 
      
  • Bonus, once. The first time a set is assembled it receives a one-time basket of about $12 (SpaceX, Google, Meta and Microsoft), in the same transaction as the last piece, paid by the mint like every other basket. Once per set, ever.
    • 
      
  • Fuse, if you choose. An assembled set can be fused for good: its pieces can never leave the Face that holds them, the whole face gets a neon frame, and the set only ever changes hands whole. It can't be undone.
    • 
      
  • A say. Before funding new baskets the treasury asks the assembled sets: one set, one vote. Advice it publishes and reads, not a rule.

Wallet

      
Every Face is a wallet

      

        
Each Face gets its own account on the chain (the ERC-6551 standard): the NFT is the key. Whoever holds the Face controls the account. Tokens, stablecoins and pieces of tokenized stocks can live inside it.

        
Every Face starts with a base basket of about $5 in one of TSLA, NVDA, AAPL, AMZN or MSFT. You can leave it there, add more, or withdraw it at any time from the Face's page. If the Face changes hands, what's inside goes with it.

      

    

    

      
The mint pays for itself

      
Said before, not after

      

        
The mint happens on OpenSea. OpenSea keeps its 10% fee, and the rest goes to a split written into the contract. 55% of it goes to a seed vault: a contract that can only buy the basket tokens, at prices checked against Chainlink, and deliver them straight to the Faces' pool. That money can't be spent on anything else.

        
The shares and the addresses are fixed in the contract. Nobody can change them, and anyone can trigger the payout.

      

      

      
    

      

      
55%Seed vault: buys what goes inside the Faces

      
15%Treasury: art, site, audits, presence

      
15%Team: released slowly over 6 months, so no dumping on the floor

      
15%Growth and collaborations

    

      
Most Faces are born with their basket. A Face minted in the first minutes, before the pool is restocked, gets it shortly after; anyone can deliver a pending basket with one click from the Face's page.

      
Resale royalties are 5%, suggested and optional: transfers are never restricted.

    

    

      
Agents

      
The stare can work

      

        
Robinhood Chain is built for agents too. From your Face's page you can give it a simple rule, and the NEONFACES strategy agent runs it for you: buy one ticker every week, keep part of the Face in USDG, or trim a ticker that grew too big. Every move is written in the Face's journal with its reason. You can also bring your own agent (a bot, an app or an AI service), or simply ask your AI assistant about your Face: it reads the chain and prepares actions that you confirm in your wallet. You stay the owner.

      

      
    
      
  • It can only do the actions you allow, like trading the Face's tokens.
    • 
      
  • Trades go through a guard contract: what it buys always comes back into the Face, never more than 2% worse than the Chainlink price, and never beyond the daily limit you set.
    • 
      
  • It can spend ETH only up to the budget you give it.
    • 
      
  • It can never sign for the Face, it expires on the date you choose, and it stops the moment the Face is sold.
    • 
      
  • Strategies follow simple rules: they don't predict prices and promise nothing.
    • 
    

    

    

      
Buying and selling safely

      
Lock before you list

      

        
A seller could empty a Face's wallet right before a sale goes through. NEONFACES prevents that: the holder can lock the wallet until a date. Until then nothing can leave it, not by the holder, not by an agent, not by a signature, not even from the set pieces sitting inside it, and the lock stays in place after the sale. Buyers see "Locked Until" in the traits, and the Face page lists any token approval left open.

Built to last without us

      
It runs by itself

      

        
The art, the wallets, the Gaze and the metadata live on the chain and need nobody. The moving parts are two small programs: a keeper that restocks and delivers the baskets, pays the set bonuses and the split, and tells marketplaces once a day to refresh the traits; and the strategy agent that runs the strategies holders chose. They run on a free schedule; every keeper step can be done by anyone from a block explorer, and any holder can stop the strategy agent at any time, so nothing depends on a single person or server. People are needed only for real decisions: the launch, the reveal and renewing the domain.
