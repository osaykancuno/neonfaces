01 / Lore

          

            
On Robinhood Chain the market never closes. Someone has to keep watching.

            

              
NEONFACES are not portraits of people. They are the faces of agents. No bodies. They don't need them: an eye, a nose, a strip of forehead. Everything else is out of frame. The crop is this tight because the only thing that matters is the ticker.

              
Every Face is born with a Token Bound Account. Inside that wallet lives a piece of the real market: Stock Tokens, USDG, whatever the holder or the agent puts there. Sell the Face and you sell the stare, and everything it has accumulated.

              
First there were portraits. Then PFPs. Then pixel brokers with stocks in their pockets. NEONFACES strips the costume. What's left is zero distance: skin reduced to blocks, light reduced to #CCFF00, identity reduced to an account.

              
5555 is not random. Five, like the fingers holding a phone open all night. Five thousand and more, because a 24/7 market needs more than one watcher. Some faces are darker. Some carry more neon. That's not cosmetic rarity: it's how long that stare has stayed on.

            

            
They don't smile. They don't pose. They don't blink.

          

        


        

          
02 / Native to the chain

          
Three things. No jargon.

          
    
            
  1. 
              Every Face has its own wallet.
              
    Not an image sitting in an account: the image is the key to an account. Tokens, stablecoins and pieces of tokenized stocks can live inside it.
    
            
    2. 
            
  2. 
              A Face is never left empty.
              
    The mint pays for what's inside: part of every sale can only buy a small, visible basket of Stock Tokens for the Faces. Most Faces are born with it; one minted before the pool is restocked gets it minutes later. Leave it there, add to it, or withdraw it. If the Face changes hands, what's inside follows.
    
            
    3. 
            
  3. 
              The stare can work.
              
    This chain is built for agents too. You stay the owner and give it a simple rule: accumulate a ticker, keep part of it in USDG, trim a ticker that grew too big. The NEONFACES strategy agent runs it for you and writes the reason for every move in the Face's journal. You can also ask your AI assistant about your Face: it reads the chain and prepares actions you confirm.
    
            
    4. 
          

          

            Plain truth
            
We don't sell real shares or shareholder rights. We sell an artwork that can hold on-chain exposure.

          

          

            

              
Stare tiers

              
Every Face gets a base basket at mint. At reveal the art decides the tier (Glance 4444, Watch 833, Heavy Stare 278 among the 5555 artworks) and Watch / Heavy Stare Faces receive a top-up: a ticker plus USDG, or a richer basket. Nobody can know a tier inside the mint transaction, so bots can't snipe them.

            

            

              
The Gaze

              
The longer a Face stays with one holder, the more its neon burns: after 30, 90 and 365 days the art itself blooms (Steady, Fixed, Burning), drawn on-chain from the Unblinking clock. Selling resets it. Patience shows in the picture, not in a payout.

            

            

              
Lock before you list

              
A holder can lock the account until a date: nothing leaves it (not the holder, not the agent, no signature) and the lock survives the sale. Buyers see "Locked until" in the metadata and get exactly what they paid for.

04 / Sets

          
Four pieces. One face.

          
555 faces were cut in four: left eye, right eye, left mouth, right mouth. Each piece is a Face of its own, with its own account. Half of them are women, half are men, and the four pieces of a set share its Stare tier.

          

          
    
            
  1. Collect.
    The reveal deals whole sets to random tokens, so every set that exists can be completed. Nobody is dealt a finished one: the pieces land far apart. Each piece shows Set #n and its part in the metadata, so you can make an offer on exactly the piece you miss.
    2. 
            
  2. Assemble.
    Move the other three pieces into one piece's account. That Face now shows the whole face, and whoever buys it gets all four pieces in one sale. Take it apart whenever you like.
    3. 
            
  3. Bonus, once.
    The first time a set is assembled, it receives a one-time bonus basket of Stock Tokens, paid by the mint like the seeds. Once per set, ever: taking it apart and assembling it again earns nothing more.
    4. 
          

          

            
Set hunter

            
Hold a piece? See where the other three are, who holds them and for how long. Watch a set and the next visit tells you which of its pieces changed hands. Read from the chain; listings are on OpenSea, one click away.

07 / Different by construction

          
Everything here is checkable on-chain.

          
A busy chain means a lot of drops that look alike. Every line below is enforced by the contracts, not by a promise.

          

            
Typical dropNEONFACES

            
ArtAn image on IPFS or a server that can go darkPixels stored on-chain and drawn by the contract itself, sealed against a hash committed before mint

            
WalletNone, or an empty oneAn ERC-6551 account created in the mint transaction and filled by the mint itself: the seed share can only buy the Faces' Stock Tokens

            
RarityAn off-chain script and a spreadsheetPublished exact counts; each token's art and tier set at reveal by an on-chain permutation anyone can recompute

            
MetadataFrozen JSON, stale on day twoLive: traits read what the Face actually holds

            
Sets"Collect them all" with no way to check555 four-piece faces. The contract knows every set, deals only whole ones, shows an assembled set as one face and pays its bonus once

            
HoldingNothing rewards patienceUnblinking: an on-chain clock of how long the stare has stayed with its holder; after 30, 90 and 365 days the neon blooms in the art itself. Selling resets it.

            
AgentsA slideDelegation built into every account (allowed calls only, a budget you set, no signatures, expires, dies on sale), ready-made strategies run for you, and a public reason for every move

            
ResaleSeller can empty the wallet before the sale landsAccount lock that survives the sale, visible in the metadata

            
ControlUpgradeable proxy, pausable transfersNo proxy, no transfer pause, no blocklist. Supply fixed in bytecode. Safe multisig with a 2-day handover.

          

        


        

          
08 / Where the mint goes

          
Said before, not after.

          
A major share turns the Faces on: the content of their wallets. A share stays in the treasury for art, site, presence and liquidity. A share is the team's, with rules against dumping on the floor. A share goes to growth and collaborations. If the Faces were born empty, NEONFACES would be just an aesthetic. The aesthetic is strong. It isn't enough.

          

            
            
    
          
    
          
    The mint runs on OpenSea, which keeps 10% of primary sales. The rest can only be paid to NeonPayout: shares and addresses are immutable, and anyone can push the split with releaseAll(). Resale royalties: 5% suggested on-chain (ERC-2981) to the treasury multisig, optional because transfers are never restricted.
    
        
    

        
    
          
    09 / Principles
    
          
    How this lasts. And how it fails.
    
          
    
            
    
              
    It works if
    
              
      
                
    • the art stays iron-clad and recognizable
      • 
                
    • the Face lit at mint is real, not a slide
      • 
                
    • the community is chosen, not inflated
      • 
                
    • the tone stays adult: market, stare, neon, no mascots
      • 
              
    
            
    
            
    
              
    It fails if
    
              
      
                
    • it becomes "yet another pixel broker"
      • 
                
    • it promises returns
      • 
                
    • it grows the supply or the allowlist out of fear (the supply is 5555 in bytecode: it can't grow even if we wanted it to)
      • 
                
    • the team disappears after mint and only the listings feed is left
      • 
              
    
            
    
          
    
          
    The reference isn't the biggest collection on the chain. It's the one that proved a portrait can carry a piece of the market. NEONFACES takes that intuition and moves it to art: less character, more close-up, the chain's color, a stare instead of a costume.

    12 / FAQ
    
          
    
            
    Where do I mint?
    On OpenSea, on Robinhood Chain: the button above opens the drop. This site is where you look inside your Face and manage it: withdraw, lock, delegate an agent.
    
            
    What is actually inside a Face?
    An ERC-6551 account controlled by whoever holds the NFT: a base basket from mint, a top-up after reveal for Watch / Heavy Stare, plus anything you add. You can withdraw everything, add more, delegate an agent or lock it, all from the Face's page.
    
            
    Are these shares?
    No. You buy an artwork that can hold on-chain exposure. Stock Tokens are tokenized instruments that track the underlying; they give no legal ownership and no shareholder rights.
    
            
    When do I know my Stare tier?
    At reveal, after the mint closes. The reveal maps every token to one art piece with a keyed permutation (check it with artIdOf(tokenId)) and the art decides the tier. Watch / Heavy Stare top-ups can then be delivered by anyone, in one call.
    
            
    What is the Gaze?
    Every Face counts how long it has stayed with its current holder (Unblinking). After 30 days its neon starts to bloom (Steady), after 90 it is Fixed, after a year it is Burning. The glow is drawn by the contract from that clock, so it shows everywhere the Face is shown. A sale resets it: the stare belongs to whoever keeps it.
    
            
    How do sets work?
    2220 Faces are the pieces of 555 whole faces. After the reveal each piece shows its set and part. Hold all four, then press Assemble on the Face that should carry the set: the other three move into its account (three transfers from your wallet). That Face shows the whole face and gets the set's one-time bonus basket. To take it apart, press Take apart. If the mint doesn't sell out, fewer sets are dealt, but only whole ones.
    
            
    What can an agent do with my Face?
    Only what you allow, within the ETH and daily limits you set. Trades go through a guard contract: everything bought comes back into the Face, never more than 1% below the Chainlink price. An agent can never sign for the account, it expires, and it stops the moment the Face changes hands.
    
            
    Do I need my own bot?
    No. On your Face's page pick a strategy (buy a ticker every week, keep 40% in USDG, trim a ticker above 50%) and the NEONFACES strategy agent runs it, within the daily limit you choose. Each trade appears in the Face's journal with the reason. Stop it with one click; selling the Face stops it too. Strategies follow simple rules: they don't predict prices and promise nothing.
    
            
    Can I ask an AI about my Face?
    Yes. Give your assistant neonfaces.xyz/llms.txt, or add the NEONFACES MCP kit to Claude Desktop or any MCP client. It reads what your Face holds, its set, Gaze and agent, and prepares actions as links: you open the link, read the summary and confirm in your own wallet. It never touches your keys.
    
            
    What if the seed pool runs out?
    Minting never fails because of seeding. The seed is marked pending and delivered as soon as the pool is restocked, usually within minutes; anyone can deliver it with one call from the Face's page. The pool is restocked by the mint itself: 40% of what reaches the split goes to a vault that can only buy the basket tokens for the pool. On weekends, when stock prices don't update, restocking waits for Monday.
