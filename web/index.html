01 / Lore

          

            
On Robinhood Chain the market never closes. Someone has to keep watching.

            

              
NEONFACES are not portraits of people. They are the faces of agents. No bodies — they don't need them: an eye, a nose, a strip of forehead. Everything else is out of frame. The crop is this tight because the only thing that matters is the ticker.

              
Every Face is born with a Token Bound Account. Inside that wallet lives a piece of the real market: Stock Tokens, USDG, whatever the holder or the agent puts there. Sell the Face and you sell the stare — and everything it has accumulated.

              
First there were portraits. Then PFPs. Then pixel brokers with stocks in their pockets. NEONFACES strips the costume. What's left is zero distance: skin reduced to blocks, light reduced to #CCFF00, identity reduced to an account.

              
5555 is not random. Five, like the fingers holding a phone open all night. Five thousand and more, because a 24/7 market needs more than one watcher. Some faces are darker. Some carry more neon. That's not cosmetic rarity: it's how long that stare has stayed on.

            

            
They don't smile. They don't pose. They don't blink.

          

        


        

          
02 / Native to the chain

          
Three things. No jargon.

          
    
            
  1. 
              Every Face has its own wallet.
              
    Not an image sitting in an account: the image is the key to an account. Tokens, stablecoins and pieces of tokenized stocks can live inside it.
    
            
    2. 
            
  2. 
              A Face is never born empty.
              
    Part of the mint turns the stare on: a small, visible basket, different from piece to piece. Leave it there, add to it, or withdraw it. If the Face changes hands, what's inside follows.
    
            
    3. 
            
  3. 
              The stare can work.
              
    This chain is built for agents too. You stay the owner and can delegate simple rules — "keep part of it liquid", "accumulate one ticker". Not a landing-page bot: the same idea as a face that never closes its eyes.
    
            
    4. 
          

          

            Plain truth
            
We don't sell real shares or shareholder rights. We sell an artwork that can hold on-chain exposure.

          

          

            
          

          

            

              
How the seed works

              
The mint transaction creates the Face's account through the canonical ERC-6551 registry and drops a small basket of Stock Tokens in it. Open the Face and it already holds exposure.

            

            

              
Stare tiers

              
Glance 80% — a sliver of one ticker. Watch 15% — a ticker plus USDG. Heavy Stare 5% — a richer basket. Drawn on-chain from an exact urn: 4444 / 833 / 278, no more, no less.

            

            

              
Agents, on a leash

              
Delegate an agent to your Face: it may only call the contracts and functions you allow, can't move ETH, can't sign, expires, and dies automatically the moment the Face is sold.

            

            

              
Liquid, always

              
The holder can empty the account at any time. The NFT stays the Face; the value inside is liquid. Nothing is locked, nothing is custodial.

06 / Where the mint goes

          
Said before, not after.

          
A major share turns the Faces on — the content of their wallets. A share stays in the treasury for art, site, presence and liquidity. A share is the team's, with rules against dumping on the floor. A share goes to growth and collaborations. If the Faces were born empty, NEONFACES would be just an aesthetic. The aesthetic is strong. It isn't enough.

          

            
            
    
          
    
          
    Shares are immutable in NeonMinter. Anyone can push the split with releaseAll(). The team share streams through a 6-month vesting contract. Royalties: 5% on-chain (ERC-2981) to the treasury multisig.

    10 / FAQ
    
          
    
            
    What is actually inside a Face?
    An ERC-6551 account controlled by whoever holds the NFT. At mint it receives a small basket of Stock Tokens (and USDG for Watch / Heavy Stare). You can add more, withdraw everything, or delegate a scoped agent.
    
            
    Are these shares?
    No. You buy an artwork that can hold on-chain exposure — not shares, not shareholder rights. Stock Tokens are tokenized instruments that give economic exposure to the underlying; they are not legal ownership of the shares. They are not available to US persons. We sell "on-chain exposure held in an account bound to the Face", never "shares inside an NFT".
    
            
    What if the seed pool runs out?
    Minting never fails because of seeding. The seed is marked pending and anyone can fund it later with one call once the pool is refilled. 40% of mint proceeds go to the seed vault that refills it.
    
            
    How is the Stare tier assigned?
    On-chain at mint, from an exact urn: the probability of each tier is its remaining slots over the remaining supply, so the final count is exactly 4444 / 833 / 278.
    
            
    How does the reveal work?
    Art is committed by hash before mint. After mint, a future L2 block hash sets a random offset inside each tier. Every token's art is then computable by anyone with artIdOf(tokenId).
    
            
    Can the team change the art or pause trading?
    No. The art contract is sealed against the provenance hash. There is no transfer pause, no blocklist, no upgradeable proxy. The admin is a Safe multisig with a 2-day handover delay.
    
            
    Royalties?
    5% on-chain via ERC-2981, capped in the contract, paid to the treasury multisig.
